# The cold reader sees only SKILL.md. Each question has one expected answer.
Q: How must an R item in the Requirements section of a PR relate to its issue?
A: The R item text equals the issue item after white space is collapsed, the R items keep the issue order, and the PR adds none and drops none.

Q: What may an evidence line hold?
A: A path that the PR diff changes (or a folder with such a file), an `https://` URL, or `` `command` -> result ``. A commit SHA is never evidence.

Q: Why is a commit SHA not accepted as evidence or as a fix?
A: The push rules require squash and `--force-with-lease`, so each rebase changes the SHAs.

Q: May the shepherd reject a review finding on its own?
A: It may reject a bot finding alone, but before it rejects a human finding it must ask the user and pass `--confirmed-by-user` after the user agrees.

Q: When does the check fail on the trace?
A: The check fails when the head commit has no `pr-shepherd/trace` status, when the status is not `success`, or when a hash in its description differs.

Q: What must the rules of the base branch require before `shepherd.sh merge` turns on auto-merge?
A: The rules of the base branch must require the `pr-shepherd` check.

Q: Which pull requests are exempt from the rules?
A: Dependabot PRs are exempt from every rule. A PR with the label `pr:no-issue` needs no Requirements section and no trace, but its checkboxes still need ticks and evidence and its findings still need triage.

Q: What does the shepherd do with a trace row marked uncertain?
A: The row passes, but the shepherd sharpens the sub-tasks or the evidence and runs verify again. If the row stays uncertain, the shepherd decides itself and states the reason in the `## Notes` section of the PR.

Q: What does the shepherd do when another check of the PR, such as a CI job, fails?
A: It runs `shepherd.sh checks <pr> --watch`, reads the failed log, fixes the cause, pushes and starts a new pass, and it never re-runs a failed check to hide a real failure.
