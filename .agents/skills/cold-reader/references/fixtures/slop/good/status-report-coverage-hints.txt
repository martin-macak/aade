## Coverage check: passed, 3 of 12 packages need a review

This check does not block the merge. A reviewer reads each row under "Needs review".

- **Line coverage:** the share of lines that the tests run.
- **Branch coverage:** the share of branches that the tests take.

A check fails below 60 %. From 80 % on, it passes without a review. Each row under "Needs review" gives the hint of each check that scored from 60 % to 80 %:

- Line coverage: the tests skip some new lines of the package.
- Branch coverage: the tests skip some error branches of the package.
