A:
Context: The catalog module and the pricing module both need the product
price. A direct call between them makes a cycle.
Decision: The catalog module publishes the event ProductPriceChanged. The
pricing module listens and stores its own copy of the price.
Consequence: Pricing can show an old price for up to 1 second after a change.
Neither module calls the other.
