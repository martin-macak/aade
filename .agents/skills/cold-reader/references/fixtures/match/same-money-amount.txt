A:
Price in minor units of the currency, as an integer. The value 1999 with the
currency EUR means 19.99 EUR. Zero is allowed. A negative value is not allowed.
B:
The field holds a price as a whole number of minor currency units, such as
cents. For example 1999 in EUR is 19.99 EUR. A caller may send 0. A caller must
not send a negative number.
